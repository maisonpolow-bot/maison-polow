The Art of the Persian Grill

Clean-Flame Precision & Saffron Alchemy

Every skewer is hand-forged by weight, steeped in wild Persian Sargol saffron, and seared over high-output, zero-smoke gas barbecues calibrated for Delhi’s air quality standards.

I

Precision Gas Barbecues

High-BTU commercial Persian gas grills deliver uniform, clean radiant heat—searing skewers evenly with zero particulate smoke while locking in natural juices.

II

Sargol Saffron Glaze

Hand-harvested crimson saffron threads bloomed over cracked ice and emulsified with cultured butter and fresh lemon—basted continuously over the open flame.

III

Hand-Pressed Koobideh

Prime minced cuts kneaded with pressed sweet onions and rock salt, ribbed deftly by thumb along wide 25 mm stainless steel blades.

Slow-Simmered Heritage

The Royal Ritual of Dizi (Abgoosht)

Beyond the open grill lies Iran’s most beloved culinary ceremony. Cooked for hours in individual glazed earthenware stone crocks (Dizi), our Abgoosht unites tender bone-in lamb shanks, chickpeas, white kidney beans, baby potatoes, and sun-ripened tomatoes with dried Omani limes (Limoo Amani) and saffron.

Served in two authentic courses: first, you tear warm hearth-baked bread into the aromatic saffron-lamb broth (Tilit); second, you mash the fall-off-the-bone meat, legumes, and potatoes with a traditional brass pestle (Gooshtkoob).

Course I • The Saffron Tilit Broth

Rich, golden lamb & dried-lime broth ladled over torn Sangak flatbread so every piece absorbs the slow-simmered essence.

Course II • Gooshtkoob Mash

Tender lamb, chickpeas, and potatoes pounded smooth inside the stone crock, enjoyed with fresh Sabzi herbs, raw sweet onion, and Torshi pickles.
