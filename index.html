The Persian Rice Philosophy

Rice as the Centerpiece. Honored with Patience.

We reject quick-boil shortcuts. Every grain of aged basmati is soaked, steamed, and bloomed in wild Sargol saffron to achieve celestial fluffiness.

Wild Sargol Saffron

Pure hand-harvested red saffron threads bloomed in warm spring water, imparting a radiant golden hue and delicate floral aroma across every single long grain.

The Golden Tahdig

The prized crisp golden rice crust formed at the bottom of the heavy-bottomed pot—a textural masterpiece shared among honoured guests at the table.

Jeweled Garnishes

Platters crowned with tart ruby barberries (Zereshk), slivered pistachios, almonds, and slow-braised meats that turn every meal into a celebration.

Anatomy of Our Emblem

The Architecture of Craft & Tradition

The Maison Polow visual identity is an intricate architectural harmony reflecting centuries of Persian culinary heritage and artisanal rice preparation.

Geometry & Shield Architecture

The octagonal Persian starburst cartouche and inner segmented borders have been reconstructed with sharp, uniform strokes, eliminating overlapping artifacts and blurred transitions.

Monogram & Sunburst

The interlaced gold MP monogram stands cleanly against the radial sunburst without the flower backing, retaining smooth, deliberate curves and balanced stroke contrast.

Botanical & Culinary Compartments

Every ingredient—barberries, fava beans, golden wheat grains, green beans, lentils (adas), dill fronds, and the central bowl of steaming long-grain rice—has well-defined outlines and clean color separation.

Flanking Harvest Crest & Typography

Surrounding stalks of mature rice, foliage, and pomegranate buds feature crisp silhouettes, perfectly complemented by sharp, consistent typography rendering MAISON POLOW, PERSIAN RICE ATELIER, and TAHDIG • TAHCHIN against the warm alabaster background.
