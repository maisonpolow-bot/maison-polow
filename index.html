The Persian Baking Philosophy

Engineered for Flavour. Crafted for Digestion.

We reject chemical baking improvers and rapid yeast spikes. Every loaf honors three centuries of Iranian hearth science.

24-Hour Fermentation

Slow cold retardation breaks down complex starches and phytic acid. The result is a feather-light crumb that sits easily on the stomach without post-meal lethargy.

The "Roomal" Glaze

Before entering the live stone hearth, every loaf is brushed with a scalding flour-water glaze that locks in internal moisture and blooms into a crackling bronze crust.

Live Stone Firing

Direct contact with blazing stone decks produces intense oven spring, hollow blister pockets, and toasted nigella and sesame aroma across every groove.
