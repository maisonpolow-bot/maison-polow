Our Philosophy

Rice as the Centerpiece

In many culinary traditions, rice is a quiet accompaniment—a background note to a heavy curry or gravy. At Maison Polow, we flip that perspective entirely. Here, rice is the undisputed hero of the table.

Born from a passion to introduce Delhi’s curious food explorers to the elegance of Persian cuisine, Maison Polow explores the nuanced artistry of long-grain basmati, rare aromatic herbs, wild Persian saffron, and slow-braised treasures.

Every dish tells a story of patience, precise steam-cooking, and the coveted golden crust known as tahdig. We invite you to slow down, gather close, and discover a world where every single grain is treated with reverence.

The Persian Table Tradition

Dining in Persia is communal and generous. Platters are shared across the table, balancing tart barberries with sweet dates, savory lamb, and buttery aromatic rice.

Crafted for Delhi Diners

Whether you are a seasoned connoisseur of Middle Eastern flavors or experiencing Persian rice cooking for the very first time, our atelier offers an approachable yet deeply authentic journey.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Polow refers to layered or mixed Persian rice dishes where ingredients like herbs, lentils, fava beans, fruits, or meats are cooked with or layered into aromatic basmati rice, rather than served as a plain side.
Each dish features unique aromatics and regional heritage. For instance, Zereshk Polo highlights tart barberries, Baghali Polo pairs dill and fava beans with lamb, and Tahchin is a baked saffron yogurt rice cake.
Certain rice preparations can be tailored for vegetarian guests, though traditional stocks or suggested accompaniments may include meat or poultry. Please check individual dish notes or inquire directly for specific dietary options.
Some celebratory rice dishes incorporate almonds, pistachios, yogurt, butter, or eggs (such as Tahchin). We encourage guests with specific allergies to consult our team before ordering as we finalize kitchen operations.
